My Journey in the World of Programming

Matvey Podolyan

I am a developer and writer. I work with JavaScript, study coding, web development, and cybersecurity. I previously worked with Python. I am just starting my journey as a developer and programmer, with the goal of becoming an expert in my field. My life goal is to make technology simple to understand and interesting to learn. You can explore my GitHub and my work here: GitHub And here’s how it all started... On April 14, 2026, Dad recommended that I study code and create websites. He showed me examples of cool websites I could also create. I started learning about "tags," "prefixes," and the like, and there were many trials, attempts, and failures. But after many attempts, I learned. After I started learning what "style.css" was, it became much easier for me to work with code. I didn't have to move anything around or paste it multiple times, which really helped me. Then I learned the basics of HTML and CSS, for example, headings, blocks, paragraphs, and links. Honestly, it wasn't easy to remember, but over time, I began to understand these concepts better. Then I started learning JS includes. At first, I didn't understand how they worked, but then I realized they were simply a way to connect code to the site, and it was great because I could write the code in one place and have it work on all pages. The next step was making copies of my pages, only in English. This was difficult because I had to translate all the text and understand how it worked, but I managed it, and now I have two versions of my site, one in Russian and one in English. After a while, I started learning different concepts, like "Footer," various menus, and other cool tricks every beginner should know, like how to insert images. After 2-3 weeks, I made significant progress and now feel confident in my knowledge. And I intend to continue my journey in the world of programming. It's really interesting!
